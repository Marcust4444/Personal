# Oct–Dec Training Block

Phone workout logger for the Mon 19 Oct – Fri 25 Dec 2026 training plan. It covers Upper B, Lowers, Upper A, Full Body and the cardio days. You log weights and reps per set, and it suggests progressive overload for the next session.

- **Log**: today's session in superset groups. Suggested weights show as grey placeholders, and tapping ✓ fills them in and starts the rest timer.
- **Overload**: hit every rep and it adds 2.5 kg (5 kg on squat, RDL, hip thrust and calves). Miss reps and it holds the weight. Miss two sessions in a row and it suggests a 10% deload. Rep ranges use double progression.
- **% lifts**: bench and incline sets are calculated from your 1RM, and it offers a +2.5 kg 1RM bump once every rep is clean.
- **Plate maths**: shows the plates per side for barbell lifts.
- **Plan / Progress**: a 10-week calendar, estimated-1RM charts, PRs and total volume.
- Works offline once installed. Data stays on the phone, and Setup has a backup export and import.

## Install on your phone
1. Turn on GitHub Pages: repo **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch**, pick this branch and `/ (root)`.
2. Open `https://marcust4444.github.io/Personal/` on your phone.
3. iPhone: Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**. Android: Chrome → ⋮ → **Install app**.
